Protein-Based Fish Species Identification: Dataset, Models, and Insights from Native Bangladeshi Fish

N. H. Fahim, A. U. Muhib and M. S. Rahman. 2026 IEEE 2nd International Conference on Quantum Photonics, Artificial Intelligence, and Networking (QPAIN), Chittagong, Bangladesh, 2026, pp. 1–6.

Contribution
Curated a study dataset of 2,845 protein sequences spanning nine native Bangladeshi fish species and compared seven architectures under a shared evaluation.
Result
A motif-aware CNN-Transformer with type-aware positional encoding reached 79.80% accuracy with CPU-capable inference, running 5× faster and using a model 42× smaller than the ProtBERT baseline.
Why it matters
The work examines species identification in a resource-constrained setting where deployment cost is part of the research problem.
DOI: 10.1109/QPAIN69676.2026.11546620

Real-Time Earthquake Magnitude Classification from Initial P-Waves: Models, Dataset, and Comparative Analysis for South Asia

M. N. Hasan Fahim, M. A. Ullah Muhib, R. A. Tanvir and A. Al Noman. 2025 28th International Conference on Computer and Information Technology (ICCIT), Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh, 2025, pp. 5175–5180.

Contribution
Curated 7,318 South Asian earthquake events for single-station early-warning experiments and compared six model families using seven-second post-P-wave windows.
Result
The Transformer reached 76.23% accuracy, 81.56% adaptive accuracy, and 4.8 ms inference latency under the study protocol.
Why it matters
The study provides a controlled regional comparison for fast magnitude classification from limited initial waveform information.
DOI: 10.1109/ICCIT68739.2025.11489542
