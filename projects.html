Full-stack

Multimodal AI search & recommendation engine

Can a shopper find a product by describing it and showing a photograph of something similar, in the same query?

A full-stack e-commerce platform built around a hybrid retrieval microservice. OpenAI Vision produces embeddings for product imagery, ChromaDB serves nearest-neighbour search over combined text and image vectors, and a PostgreSQL-to-vector synchronisation pipeline keeps the index consistent with the catalogue as it changes — which is the part that is actually hard. Redis caches hot queries; the whole thing is containerised with Docker.

Computer vision

COWTRAIN — cattle weight estimation from photographs

Livestock is priced by weight, and a scale is expensive. Can a phone photograph do the job?

An end-to-end pipeline: YOLOv8 instance segmentation isolates the animal and a reference object of known size in the same frame, the pixel-to-metric scale is recovered from the reference, and a regression model maps the resulting morphometry to weight. Served behind a FastAPI backend. The reference-object trick is what makes it work from an uncalibrated camera at an unknown distance.

Gamified social platform for BRAC

Backend engineering during the BRAC Tech & SELP-RHRN AI fellowship, on a platform addressing gender-discrimination attitudes. I built the FastAPI backend, multi-LLM pipelines over GPT‑4o and Gemini serving real-time Bengali content — book and research-paper search with suggestions — and PostgreSQL JSONB schemas for semi-structured content, deployed as containerised microservices. Working Bengali-language LLM systems at NGO scale is a different problem from working English ones at demo scale.
