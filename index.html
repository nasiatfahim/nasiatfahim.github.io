Ongoing

Protein language models

I adapt ProGen2 toward thermophile-associated sequence space using parameter-efficient supervised fine-tuning and preference optimisation. The work compares likelihood and preference objectives under matched compute and homology-controlled evaluation, then uses attribution methods to examine the biophysical signals learned by the adapted model.

Ongoing

Earth observation and SAR water mapping

I develop Sentinel-1 water-segmentation models and study how geographic domain shift, biome differences, radar backscatter, speckle, shadow, and layover affect cross-region reliability. The objective is to identify when a segmentation model should not be trusted, not only to improve its average score.

Published + ongoing

Earthquake early warning

My published study curated 7,318 South Asian earthquake events and compared six models using seven-second, single-station P-wave windows. The current direction studies cross-region transfer and links early waveform information with shaking, energy, and source properties while checking model behaviour through explainability.
