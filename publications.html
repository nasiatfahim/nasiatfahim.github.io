Protein-Based Fish Species Identification: Dataset, Models, and Insights from Native Bangladeshi Fish

N. H. Fahim, A. U. Muhib and M. S. Rahman. 2026 IEEE 2nd International Conference on Quantum Photonics, Artificial Intelligence, and Networking (QPAIN), Chittagong, Bangladesh, 2026, pp. 1–6.

Contribution
Built and released a dataset of 2,845 protein sequences spanning nine native Bangladeshi fish species, then benchmarked seven architectures on it — there was no existing benchmark for this task on regional species.
Result
A motif-aware CNN–Transformer with type-aware positional encoding reached 79.80% accuracy while running on CPU alone: 5× faster and 42× smaller than the ProtBERT baseline.
Why it matters
Species verification is a food-fraud and fisheries-management problem in settings where GPU inference is not available. Model size was a design constraint, not an afterthought.
DOI: 10.1109/QPAIN69676.2026.11546620

Real-Time Earthquake Magnitude Classification from Initial P-Waves: Models, Dataset, and Comparative Analysis for South Asia

M. N. Hasan Fahim, M. A. Ullah Muhib, R. A. Tanvir and A. Al Noman. 2025 28th International Conference on Computer and Information Technology (ICCIT), Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh, 2025, pp. 5175–5180.

Contribution
Curated 7,318 South Asian earthquake events into a benchmark for single-station early warning, then compared six model families on seven-second post-P-wave windows under identical conditions.
Result
The Transformer reached 76.23% accuracy and 81.56% adaptive accuracy at 4.8 ms inference latency — comfortably inside the budget a real warning system has between detection and shaking.
Why it matters
South Asia is densely populated, seismically active and underrepresented in early-warning benchmarks. Models tuned on Japanese or Californian networks should not be assumed to transfer here, and this dataset makes that testable.
DOI: 10.1109/ICCIT68739.2025.11489542
